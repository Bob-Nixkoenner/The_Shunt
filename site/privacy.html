Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. Applies to the app The Shunt (package org.theshunt.app) and the Linux and Windows versions.

In short

The Shunt has no accounts, no servers of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. The developer receives no data from your use of the app. Settings, keys and statistics stay on your device.

Controller

Bob Nixkoenner, address see legal notice. Contact: shunt@isst.pizza

What the app does with the VPN permission

The app uses Android's VPN service to send traffic of the apps you choose through tunnels you configure (WireGuard, OpenVPN, Tailscale/Headscale). The app decides per app, address, port or domain which tunnel is used. This happens on your device. The traffic content is not read, stored or passed on by the developer. It is sent, encrypted by the tunnel protocol, to the server you entered. That server's operator (for example your VPN provider) processes it under their own privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

  • Tunnel configurations, private keys and pre-shared keys that you add.
  • Rules and app selections.
  • Counters: transferred bytes per tunnel and number of blocked DNS requests, kept in a local file for the history chart. No list of visited sites is kept.

Nothing of this leaves the device unless you export it yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Logs

The app writes short technical messages to the Android system log (for example "start failed"). They contain no traffic content. They are not sent anywhere by the app.

No sharing, no sale

No data is sold or shared with third parties by the developer. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to disclose or erase on the developer's side. Under the GDPR you still have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and to complain to a supervisory authority, for example the one of your federal state.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 16.
